## Anti-Patterns Verdict

Does not read as AI-made. Serif headings with a sans body, one green accent, no gradients, no glass, no hero metrics.
Possible tells: the monospace "For SF State students" kicker on the landing (one, so voice rather than scaffold) and a
four-card feature grid on the landing (icon, title, text).
Detector: 2 warnings. The play-icon triangle flagged as a side-tab border (index.css:2047) is a CSS triangle, false
positive. "Single font" is false: index.html loads Source Serif 4, Source Sans 3 and Source Code Pro.

## What's Working
- Plain words on the plan: "You can graduate Fall 2030", "27 of 27 requirements are complete", warnings in sentences.
- The chat shows each change as one line with Show in plan and Undo, and says what is left of the hourly limit.
- Contrast: no text under its threshold on the Plan screen; nothing under 12 px.

## Priority Issues
- [P2] The workspace has no <main> landmark (record, review and goals screens do): screen-reader users cannot jump to the plan. Fix: wrap the workspace's main column in <main>. /impeccable harden
- [P2] Every row of the Major electives group says "Electives (15 units)": five rows, each "15 units". The title shows the group's total, not the row's. Fix: say "Elective 1 of 5 · 3 units" or the row's own units. /impeccable clarify
- [P2] The Electives intro says "Swap or Choose a row to see the courses that fit" but a chosen row's button now says Details (Swap is inside it). Fix: "Open a row to see its course or the courses that fit." /impeccable clarify
- [P2] Build: with Gator AI on, the build screen sat on "about a minute" for about two minutes with no way to tell it was alive. Fix: a lengthening hint after 60 s ("Still working; you can skip") or a time-out into the plan. /impeccable harden
- [P3] The stepper hides the labels of the two non-current steps at 735 px and below, leaving bare circles. Fix: keep a short label ("Record", "Goals"). /impeccable adapt

## Persona Red Flags
**ESL transfer student:** "Plan updated · 20 courses moved · graduate Fall 2030 · checked against the Bulletin" and the AI's long bulleted reply are long for a second-language reader; the check summary is the useful part and comes last.
**Phone-only student:** at narrow width the Schedule falls back to a day list (good); the "Ask Gator AI" bar covers the last line of a scrolled page unless the page leaves room.
**Keyboard user:** the focus ring rules exist (9 :focus-visible rules) and Esc closes the cards; no skip link.

## Minor Observations
- Inline links such as "See your requirements" are 22-23 px tall (WCAG 2.2 exempts inline text links).
- 18 hard-coded hex colors sit outside the :root tokens; there is no dark mode (color-scheme: light), which is a choice, not a defect.
- z-index uses 10 different values (2 to 200) rather than a named scale.

## Questions to Consider

- Does the student ever need to see "Electives (15 units)" at all, or just "Elective 3 of 5"?
