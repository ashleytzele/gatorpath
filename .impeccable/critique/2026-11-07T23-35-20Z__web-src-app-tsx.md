## Anti-Patterns Verdict

Tells: the landing is the stock SaaS template (giant hero with a claim, sample card, three-icon trust strip, numbered three steps, "Questions, answered." ## Priority Issues

[P1] Internal names and raw engine text reach students. The plan problem panel prints "Stopped at 2030-spring: nothing more can be scheduled. Blocked: …; slots waiting: ai_cslg, ai_usg, ai_ush, ge_d_disciplines, sfss_aerm, sfss_gp, sfss_sj"; the advisor card says "GatorPath's bs-cs rules"; chat bullets say "2027-spring". Fix: map group ids to titles and terms to "Spring 2027" wherever engine text is shown; split the blocked sentence into one line per course ("MATH 227: needs MATH 226 with C or better, you have C-"). Command: clarify.

[P1] The plan page buries its answer. With any problem the date is replaced by "Fix the problem above to see your graduation date" under a red wall; even a valid plan runs ~4000 px: the planning-guide banner (repeated on every tab), 22 things to check, three advisor cards. Fix: lead with the date (provisional if needed) and next term; problems as a short list with one action each; advisor cards behind one "Talk to an advisor" disclosure; the planning-guide note once. Command: distill, then layout.

[P1] "Left out" looks done. Review shows 11 GE areas with the same green check badge as Completed, each with its own "Not on your DPR · Put it back" link. It's a call to confirm with an advisor (D33), not a finished requirement. Fix: a neutral "Not on your DPR" badge, and the left-out areas grouped once at the top of GE with one explanation and one put-back control. Command: clarify.

[P2] Promises and labels disagree. Privacy: "never your name, ID, grades or GPA" (Upload) vs "never your name, ID or GPA" (chat) vs "processed, then discarded" (landing). Time: "in 5 minutes" (landing), "about 2 minutes" (Upload), a minute-long AI fill. The Navigator link is "Ask an advisor", "Book advising in Navigator", "Talk to an advisor in Navigator", "Ask an advisor in Navigator". Fix: one true privacy sentence and one advisor label, used everywhere. Command: clarify.

[P2] Phone layout breaks in places: the header wraps into three two-line links; the bottom tab "Electives & GE" wraps; the plan timeline labels collide ("Spring '27Fall '27"); "Seats and rooms as of … Confirm in Class Search" and the footer split into flex columns. Fix: header = logo + one menu; short tab labels; timeline shows first, next and last on phones; notes as inline text. Command: adapt.

## Persona Red Flags

Jordan (first-timer): Review opens on 18 GE rows with "p.2260-2261" under each and 11 green "Left out" checks; doesn't know whether to click "Put it back". The plan's first content is a red wall naming ai_cslg. "Units per term: Roadmap" is jargon.

Sam (screen reader / keyboard): the tabs are plain buttons with no aria-selected or aria-current; the stepper doesn't mark the current step; heading levels skip h1 → h3; the desktop calendar is positioned divs with no table semantics (the section list below carries the text).

Casey (phone): header crowding; a 5300 px plan page with the fixed bottom tab bar over content; the chat opens full screen on "Working on it…" with no progress for a minute; timeline labels collide.

## Minor Observations

- "How it works" twice on the landing (header and hero).
- Method cards show both a radio circle and a check mark.
- "One of 515 schedules without time clashes": the count means nothing to a student; "Clash-free" is enough.
- Electives & GE repeats "Requirement / In your plan / Bulletin" labels in every row; a table header would do.
- The planning-guide banner and the footer say the same thing on every tab.
- Contrast is good everywhere (muted 7.5:1, faint 5.8:1, every status 5.5:1+); keep the 0.35 opacity faded calendar block to previews only.

## Questions to Consider
